About Richmond International School

Richmond International School opened its doors in 2019 as a dynamic, progressive and contemporary international school.

We provide a broad, balanced and rigorous British curriculum across the sciences, arts and humanities.

Our approach focuses on whole-person learning. Dedicated educators work closely with students to understand their individual needs and potential.

We believe academic excellence should be developed alongside personal, social and emotional growth.

Our aim is to prepare students for the future by encouraging inclusiveness, exploration, inquiry, learning and discipline.

Since opening, Richmond International School has grown into a strong school community with a welcoming family feel.

Our Vision

To nurture confident, responsible and capable young people who are prepared to make a positive contribution to the world.

Our Mission

To provide a safe, inclusive and inspiring learning environment where every student is supported to achieve their full potential.
