About Richmond International School

Welcome to Richmond International School

Nestled in the heart of Doha, Qatar, Richmond International School opened its doors in 2019. We are a dynamic, progressive and contemporary international school offering a broad, balanced and rigorous British curriculum across the sciences, arts and humanities.

Our whole-person learning experience is led by a team of dedicated educators who recognise the individual needs and potential of every student. We encourage academic excellence while supporting personal, social and emotional development.

We believe that education should prepare students for their future. Through a culture of inclusiveness, exploration, inquiry, learning and discipline, we aim to develop confident, responsible and capable young people who can make a positive contribution to the world.

Richmond International School has grown since opening while maintaining a strong family feel. We value the involvement of our students, teachers, staff and parents in building a supportive school community.

🌟

Our Vision

Our vision is that every child leaves with a strong sense of self-confidence, prepared for their next stage of education, and possessing a genuine love for learning.

Children will develop resilient character and high aspirations for their future. We believe that children's achievement is limitless, and our goal is to instil in them the confidence and capability to make a positive impact on the world.

🎯

Our Mission

Richmond International School's mission is to provide a holistic British International Education Programme, delivered in a caring learning environment that empowers children to reach their educational and personal potential whilst nurturing their self-confidence and self-esteem.
