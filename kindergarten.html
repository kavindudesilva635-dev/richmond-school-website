Welcome to Richmond

A Happy Beginning to Every Child’s Journey

At Richmond International Kindergarten – Abu Hamour Branch, we believe that the early years are an important foundation for a child's future learning and development.

Our environment is designed to help children feel safe, valued and confident while developing important social, emotional, communication and learning skills.

  • ✓ Safe and caring learning environment
  • ✓ Learning through exploration and play
  • ✓ Encouraging creativity and confidence
  • ✓ Supportive relationships with families
